About

sevenai is an AI UGC studio, not an ad account

We produce vertical video creatives for e-commerce brands that need more hooks than a traditional creator sprint can film in a week. The company is reachable at hello@sevenaii.com.

What we do

From a product URL and a written brief we generate 9:16 UGC-style ads: hooks, avatars, voice, captions. The commercial offer is the Creative Testing Sprint at $549 for 10 files during the launch window.

What we do not do

We do not manage Meta or TikTok ad accounts. We do not invent client ROAS case studies. Mockups on the homepage are format examples. Performance numbers that are not tied to a named, dated test are not published as results.

Who this is for

DTC operators and media buyers in skincare, beauty, hair, supplements, apparel, gadgets, and home who already have a store and an offer. If you need a founder-on-camera story, hire a creator. If you need ten first-three-seconds to test this week, that is the job.
