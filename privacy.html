Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

sevenai (“we”) operates https://sevenaii.com. This policy describes personal data we collect when you use the site or submit a creative brief. Contact: hello@sevenaii.com.

Data we collect

The brief form collects name, work email, store or brand website, product to promote, and optional notes. We also receive standard server logs (IP address, user agent, referring URL) from our hosting provider.

How we use it

We use brief submissions to evaluate your product, reply, and fulfill production. We do not sell personal data. We may use anonymized aggregate site metrics to improve the page.

Processors

Form submissions may be stored with our database provider (Supabase). Hosting and CDN may be provided by Vercel. The on-site assistant may send the questions you type to a language-model API in order to reply. Do not submit passwords or payment card numbers in the chat or the form.

Retention

Brief records are kept as long as needed to deliver the sprint, handle disputes, and meet legal accounting requirements, then deleted or anonymized.

Your rights

Depending on your location (including the EEA/UK and certain US states), you may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by emailing hello@sevenaii.com.

Children

The site is intended for business customers, not children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
