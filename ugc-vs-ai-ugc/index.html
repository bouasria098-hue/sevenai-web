What AI UGC is for

Media buyers need more angles than a single creator can film in a week. AI UGC is a way to fill the test matrix: same product, many first-three-seconds. Keep human creators for testimonials, unboxings you cannot fake, and brand films.

FAQ

Does a lower production cost mean lower CAC?

No. Production cost is what you pay to get files. CAC depends on the ad account, offer, and audience. More hooks to test can improve the odds of finding a winner; it does not guarantee one.

Where do the traditional UGC dollar ranges come from?

They are typical 2024–2026 US creator quote bands for a single UGC video ($250–$700+) plus extra fees for additional hooks and 30–90 day paid usage. Your quotes may differ by niche and follower count.
