Process

How sevenai turns a product link into 10 ads

sevenai is an AI creative studio. You send a product URL and a brief. We return ten 9:16 video files for TikTok Shop, Meta Reels, and YouTube Shorts, usually within 24 to 48 hours after the brief is approved.

01 — Briefing

Store URL, audience, offers

Share the product page, who buys it, and the claims you can legally make. We map pain points and reasons to buy from that page — not from a generic script template.

02 — Hook strategy

5 to 10 opening angles

Paid social dies in the first three seconds. We write distinct hooks (problem, demo, social proof, pattern interrupt) so media buyers can test angles instead of one creator take.

03 — AI generation

Avatars, voice, b-roll, captions

We generate UGC-style presenters, native-sounding voice, product-in-hand shots, and captions sized for 9:16. Brand colors, type, and logos are matched when you provide them.

04 — Delivery

10 files, commercial rights

You get downloadable video files ready for Ads Manager. Perpetual commercial usage is included. We do not promise a winning ROAS; we promise volume of testable hooks on a short clock.

What this is not

It is not a media-buying service, a TikTok Shop agency, or a guarantee that a given avatar will pass every ad-account review. Platform policies change. You remain responsible for claims in the ad.

FAQ

What do I need to send to start?

A store or product URL, the SKU to promote, audience notes, and any brand fonts, colors, or logos. Packaging photos help but shipping product samples is not required.

When does the 24–48 hour clock start?

After sevenai confirms the brief is complete in writing. Incomplete URLs or missing product facts pause the clock.
