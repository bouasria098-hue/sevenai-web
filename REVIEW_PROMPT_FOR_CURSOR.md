# SevenAI — Website Review & Context for Cursor

## Project Summary
- **Product**: sevenai (https://sevenaii.com) — AI-powered UGC video ad production for high-growth e-commerce & DTC brands.
- **Value Proposition**: Cut ad creative costs by 70% without sacrificing conversion rates. Hyper-realistic AI UGC video ads delivered in 24–48 hours for TikTok Shop, Meta Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
- **Offer**: Creative Testing Sprint — 10 vertical 9:16 video creatives for $497 ($49.70/video) with 100% perpetual commercial usage rights and zero physical product shipping.

## Main Files in this Folder
- `index.html`: Complete standalone landing page with Tailwind CSS CDN, custom glassmorphic styling, responsive layout, and embedded live assistant logic.
- `src/style.css`: Ambient dynamic mesh gradient backgrounds, animations, and glassmorphic card styles.
- `src/main.js`: Supabase form integration, mobile menu toggle, FAQ accordion, scroll reveal observers, and smooth video carousel.
- `assets/`: Mockups and visual demonstration assets.

## Review Instructions for Cursor
Please review `index.html` and `src/style.css` for:
1. **Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)**: Is the hero headline, comparison table, pricing sprint ($497), and CTA placement compelling for US DTC media buyers?
2. **Design & UX Quality**: Are the glassmorphism effects, spacing, typography, and contrast polished across mobile and desktop?
3. **Copywriting & Tone**: Is the direct-response American English sharp, concise, and focused on pain points (zero inventory shipping, rapid hook testing, perpetual rights)?
4. **Form & Tech Stack**: Verification of form fields (Name, Email, Store URL, Product, Notes) and mobile responsiveness.
